## 2.0.0

- **Core rewrite.** Rebuilt Yagen's Attributes for NeoForge 1.21.1, with shared combat rules and data pack configuration for weapons, damage multipliers, and weak points.
- **Combat.** Reworked damage types, elemental combinations, critical tiers, and all thirteen status effects. Added custom combat visuals, shields and shield-break protection, melee combos, and ranged fire rate and multishot.
- **Weapon builds.** Added a dedicated Mod collection and weapon panel. Level weapons to earn capacity, upgrade Mods with Endo, polarize slots with Forma, and double capacity with an Orokin Catalyst.
- **Loot and Rivens.** Added loot sources for Mods and upgrade materials, weapon-specific Rivens with Kuva rerolls, and gallium ore for crafting Orokin Catalysts.
- **Weapon compatibility.** Added support for selected weapons from Tennory, L_Ender's Cataclysm, Twilight Forest, and Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks.
- **World upgrades.** A new world is recommended for 2.0.0. Keep worlds that used earlier releases on their current mod version.
